Your **Vietnam eVisa is still pending**—48 hours, five days, maybe longer—and your flight is approaching. Your status page shows "Processing" or "Unfulfilled," your inbox is empty, and panic sets in. This guide explains what "pending" actually means, when delay is normal, and exactly what to do before you pay for a duplicate application.

**We are a private visa assistance service—not the Government of Vietnam.** Final approval timing is decided by Vietnamese immigration authorities. We can help track your filing, fix document issues, and escalate when your travel date is at risk.

## Quick answer: Vietnam eVisa pending timelines

| Tier (our service)      | Target window              | Still pending at 48 hours? | Action if window exceeded                             |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------- | -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **Normal**              | Within **5 business days** | Normal—early in the window | Contact support after 5 business days without outcome |
| **Urgent**              | **3 days**                 | Usually fine—wait          | Escalate if day 3 passes with no email                |
| **Super Urgent**        | **1 day**                  | Overdue—contact support    | Contact support same day if no approval               |
| **Weekends / holidays** | Not counted                | Clock may not have moved   | Add extra days; Tet can add a week or more            |

**Business days** exclude Saturdays, Sundays, and Vietnamese public holidays. That is why **48 clock hours is not two business days of review**: apply Thursday evening and the 48-hour mark lands on Saturday, before the weekend queue has moved at all.

For worked calendar examples and the holiday calendar, read [Vietnam eVisa processing time, weekends and holidays](/blog/vietnam-evisa-processing-time-2026).

## Why your eVisa may still show "pending"

### Normal immigration backlog

High-volume periods—December–January holidays, Tet (Lunar New Year), April reunification holidays, and summer peak—stretch review queues. "Pending" often means your file is in line, not lost.

### Document quality hold

Blurry passport scans, non-compliant photos, or mismatched portrait vs bio page can push your file into manual review. Check email for a **resubmission request**—typically a clearer passport scan, a replacement portrait, or confirmation of travel dates—before assuming silence means approval is coming. Status stays "Processing" until you respond.

### Payment not fully captured

Sometimes the form submits but payment fails or times out. Verify your bank statement for a completed charge, not only a temporary hold. Without confirmed payment, the application may never enter the review queue—see [payment failed](/troubleshooting/payment-failed).

### Email typo on the application

Approval emails go to the address typed on the form. A single wrong character sends your PDF elsewhere while you wait on the correct inbox. Search every folder and confirm the email address on your receipt.

### Security or eligibility screening

Some applications receive extended review with minimal public explanation. Duplicate filings, mismatched travel history, or data anomalies can add days. Contact support rather than guessing.

## What to do when pending exceeds your tier window

### Step 1: Audit your inbox and portal

- Search: your reference number, "eVisa," "immigration," "Vietnam"
- Check spam, promotions, and "Updates" tabs (Gmail)
- Log into any status-check tool with **exact surname spelling** from your passport

### Step 2: Verify payment

- Confirm charge on card or bank statement
- Match amount to your selected tier on [/fees](/fees)
- Save receipt screenshot for support

### Step 3: Contact support—do not duplicate yet

Send [support](/contact) a note like this, with your payment receipt and a portal screenshot attached:

```
Subject: eVisa pending past tier window — ref [number]

Full name (as on passport): [name]
Tier: [Normal / Urgent / Super Urgent]
Application date: [date]
Travel date and flight: [date, airline + number]
Payment: confirmed (receipt attached)
Portal status: Processing (screenshot attached)
Document resubmission email received: [yes / no]
```

**Avoid paying twice.** Duplicate applications are a top cause of extended fraud holds. See [duplicate application mistakes](/troubleshooting/duplicate-evisa-application) and [common eVisa mistakes](/blog/vietnam-evisa-common-mistakes-2026).

### Step 4: Fix document issues if requested

If immigration asked for a new photo or clearer scan, resubmit **immediately**. Pending clocks often pause until you respond.

For photo problems, see [Vietnam eVisa photo requirements](/blog/vietnam-evisa-photo-requirements-2026).

### Step 5: Escalate urgency if travel is imminent

When your flight is within **72 hours**:

- Ask whether your existing reference can be upgraded to Urgent or Super Urgent—upgrading avoids duplicate-application flags and may preserve your original payment where policy allows
- Ask whether a corrected re-filing is faster than waiting on the current file
- Prepare to reschedule if approval does not arrive before online check-in closes

## "Unfulfilled" vs "Processing" vs "Rejected"

| Status                   | Likely meaning                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Processing / Pending** | In queue or awaiting document fix                                                                                      |
| **Unfulfilled**          | Payment or required step incomplete—or portal lag; see [Unfulfilled status](/troubleshooting/evisa-unfulfilled-status) |
| **Rejected**             | Decision issued—read email for reason; usually requires new application                                                |
| **Approved**             | PDF attached—download and verify all fields                                                                            |

If status says rejected but you never received email, contact support with your reference number before re-applying.

## High-volume nationalities and realistic expectations

Indian, Chinese, American, and UK passport holders file in large numbers during peak season. Individual timelines vary even within the same tier. Your friend's two-day approval does not guarantee yours—document quality and review depth differ.

Check nationality-specific tips on [/check-requirement/india](/check-requirement/india) and your country page before escalating.

## When waiting is still the right move

- You are within your tier window on **business days**
- Payment confirmed and email address correct
- No document resubmission request outstanding
- No duplicate application filed elsewhere

If all above are true, daily status refresh is enough—escalate when the tier window passes.

## What not to do while you wait

- **Pay for a second application** without support advice
- **Assume rejection** without a rejection email
- **Dispute the card charge** while the file is under review
- **Change details on a new form**—a new photo or different entry port—while the first file is still in the queue
- **Rebook flights** before your tier window ends, unless a Super Urgent window was missed

Each of these tends to lengthen the delay, not shorten it.

## When to re-apply (only with guidance)

Re-application may be appropriate when:

- Support confirms the original filing is void or unrecoverable
- Rejection email received with no correction path
- Wrong passport number or name on an approved PDF (see [wrong name fix](/troubleshooting/wrong-name-evisa))

Always get support confirmation before a second payment.
